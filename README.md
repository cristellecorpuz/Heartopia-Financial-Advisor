# Heartopia-Financial-Advisor
Heartopia is one of my personal favorite cozy game, it features several hobbies for the players such as Cooking, Fishing, Bird Watching, and Insect Catching. I created a chatbot that players can refer to as their 'financial assistant' whereas its knowledge is limited to the obtained hobby items and their corresponding value. Users can ask questions such as "How much can I sell my blueberry jam for?" or "What is the quickest way to obtain a million coins?" and the llm will do its magic ;)
